Privacy Policy

Lease & Luxe Publisher · Last updated: November 11, 2026

What this app is

Lease & Luxe Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of Lease & Luxe (renter-friendly IKEA hacks) to upload and schedule her own videos and images to her own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook and Instagram. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What data the app accesses

  • The access tokens that each platform issues when the owner authorizes the app with her own account.
  • Basic information about the owner's own account that is needed to publish (for example the channel, page or profile ID).
  • The content the owner chooses to publish: video and image files, titles, captions and links.

The app does not access, collect or store information about any other user, follower or viewer.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the owner's own posts at the time she chooses. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Google user data

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channel. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data from the app.
